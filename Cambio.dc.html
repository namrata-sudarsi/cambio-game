Cambio

Get the lowest score to win!

HOW TO PLAY
🎯 Start with 4 face-down cards. Peek at your bottom 2, then try to get the lowest total.
🃏 Draw a card each turn, then swap it into your hand or discard to use its ability.
⚡ Stick — tap a card that matches the discard pile to remove it from your hand!
🔔 Call Cambio when you think you're lowest — opponent gets one last turn!
